## Problems Faced and How We Addressed Them

1. **Inaccurate Cutting and Tolerances**
   * *Problem:* Manual cutting using angle grinders led to dimensional inaccuracies, causing parts not to fit properly.
   * *Solution:* 
     * Introduced controlled re-measurement before each cut
     * Performed iterative trimming instead of single-step cutting
     * Increased practical tolerance margins beyond initial 1 mm assumption
     * Used 1:1 printed layout for alignment before final cuts

2. **Excess Burr Formation After Cutting**
   * *Problem:* Aluminium cutting left sharp edges and burrs, making assembly difficult and unsafe.
   * *Solution:* 
     * Introduced mandatory deburring after every cut
     * Used flat files and grinding tools for edge finishing
     * Ensured smooth mating surfaces before assembly

3. **Misalignment Due to Manual L-Brackets**
   * *Problem:* Manually cut L-brackets had inconsistent lengths, leading to misalignment in chassis assembly.
   * *Solution:* 
     * Measured brackets before installation
     * Reworked or replaced inconsistent pieces
     * Planned future designs with CAD-integrated bracket constraints

4. **Missing Mounting Considerations in CAD**
   * *Problem:* Wooden base and fixture constraints were not included in the original CAD model.
   * *Solution:* 
     * Updated assembly approach during manufacturing stage
     * Introduced custom U-clamp design for better constraint control
     * Planned future CAD models with full fixture integration

5. **Welding Fixture Stability Issues**
   * *Problem:* Required flipping the chassis during welding while maintaining structural stability.
   * *Solution:* 
     * Rejected acrylic and wooden sandwich fixture due to poor load distribution
     * Adopted aluminium extrusion-based reinforcement frame as advised by mentor
     * Ensured uniform force distribution during welding operations
